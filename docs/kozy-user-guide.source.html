1. Kozy in one page

Kozy is a holiday-rental website with two sides that work together.

The public websiteThe back office
Where guests browse properties, pick their dates, reserve, pay, and manage their stays. No account is needed to look around — only to book.

Address: the site home page 		Where the property owner publishes listings, follows reservations, records payments, and moderates guest reviews.

Address: /admin

A reservation always travels the same path:

StageWhat it meansWho moves it
PendingDates are held for the guest, payment not settled yet.Created by the guest
ConfirmedPayment received, the stay is secured.Automatic on payment
CompletedThe stay has taken place.Set by the owner
CancelledThe reservation was dropped; the nights are released.Guest or owner
Pending and confirmed reservations both block the calendar. Only a cancellation puts those nights back on sale.

PART 1For the property owner

Everything in this part happens in the back office at /admin. It is a private area — guests never see it.

2. Signing in to the back office

  1. Open /admin in your browser (for example yoursite.com/admin).
  2. Enter the email address and password of your owner account, then select Sign in.
  3. You land on the dashboard. The left sidebar holds everything else.

The sidebar is organised in three groups:

Rental managementRental properties, Bookings, Property images
RevenuePayments
PeopleClients, Reviews
Handy shortcuts. Press Ctrl+K (or Cmd+K on a Mac) anywhere to search across properties, bookings and guests by name, title or email. The sidebar can be collapsed with the toggle at its top to give tables more room. Your own name at the top right opens your profile, and Visit website there opens the public site in a new tab so you can check how your listing looks to guests.

3. The dashboard

The first screen answers "how is the business doing right now?" with six figures.

CardWhat it tells you
Revenue this monthMoney settled this month, with an up or down arrow comparing it to last month.
Awaiting paymentTotal value of reservations that are not settled yet — your follow-up list.
Average booking valueAverage across every reservation that has not been cancelled.
Bookings this monthNumber of stays starting this month.
Guests in houseHow many parties are staying at this very moment.
Reviews to moderateGuest reviews waiting for your approval before they appear publicly.
A small orange badge next to Bookings in the sidebar counts reservations still marked Pending. When that badge is visible, something is waiting for a decision.

4. Publishing a rental property

Go to Rental management → Rental properties and select New rental property. The form is split into four sections.

Property overview

TitleThe guest-facing name, e.g. "Sea-view villa in Grand Baie". The web address of the listing is built from it automatically, and follows along if you rename the property.
DescriptionThe story of the stay: the atmosphere, the surroundings, what makes it special. Bold, lists and links are available in the editor.
Price per nightThe nightly rate. Every quote a guest sees is this rate multiplied by the number of nights.
CurrencyMUR, USD, EUR, GBP, AED, MAD, KES or ZAR. Prices, payments and receipts all follow the property's currency.
Max guestsThe largest party the property sleeps comfortably. Guests cannot book for more than this.
StatusActive puts the property on the public site. Inactive takes it off and stops new reservations — use it while renovating or between seasons.

Space layout

Bedrooms, kitchens and bathrooms are not typed in — Kozy counts them from the photos you upload in the gallery (see the next section), so the numbers on the public page can never drift away from the pictures.

AmenitiesShort free tags a guest scans quickly: WiFi, workspace, washer, mountain view. Type one and press Enter.
Features & amenitiesA searchable list of standard room features; pick as many as apply.

Arrival & location

AddressThe full address shown to guests.
Latitude / LongitudeOptional. Fill both to display a real map on the listing; leave them blank and the page shows a neutral location placeholder instead.
Check-in / Check-out timeOptional. When set, they appear on the public page so guests know when to arrive and leave.

Select Create to save. An active property appears on the public site immediately, and the four most recent ones are featured on the home page.

5. The photo gallery and room layout

In the Property gallery section, select Add room photo once per room you want to show. Each entry carries:

Room typeBedroom, kitchen, bathroom, and so on. This is what drives the room counts.
Room namee.g. "Master bedroom", "Kitchenette", "Family bathroom". Two photos sharing a name count as one room; photos with no name each count as their own room.
ImageThe photo file itself.
Guest-facing captionA short line describing the photo.
CoverMark one photo as the cover. It becomes the thumbnail in search results; if none is marked, the first photo is used.
Worth getting right. If a bedroom has no photo, the listing will not claim that bedroom. Photograph every room you want counted — one clear picture per room, named consistently.

You can also reach every image through Property images in the sidebar, which is convenient for replacing a single photo without opening the whole property form.

6. Handling bookings

Rental management → Bookings lists every reservation. Each row carries a reference such as BK-00042, the property, the guest, both dates, the number of nights and guests, the total, the reservation status and the payment status.

Finding what you need

  • Filter by status to isolate pending reservations awaiting a decision.
  • Filter by property when you manage several places.
  • Filter by stay dates — "Staying from" and "Staying until" — to build an arrivals list for a given week.

Acting on a reservation

The menu at the end of each row offers View, Edit and Delete.

  • Confirm a stay — open it and set the status to Confirmed. This happens on its own as soon as the guest pays online.
  • Close a stay — set it to Completed once the guests have left.
  • Cancel — set it to Cancelled. The nights immediately return to the public calendar.

You can also create a booking yourself, for a guest who phoned or emailed. Pick the property, the guest, the dates and the party size; the total is proposed for you as nightly rate × nights, and you can adjust it before saving.

Deleting is not cancelling. Deleting erases the record and its history. To free the dates while keeping the trace, set the status to Cancelled instead.

7. Payments and revenue

Every reservation gets a payment record, referenced PM-00042, which you will find under Revenue → Payments.

Payment statusMeaning
PendingNot settled yet. This is the amount shown in "Awaiting payment".
PaidSettled. The reservation is confirmed automatically.
FailedThe card payment did not go through. The guest can try again.
RefundedMoney returned to the guest.

When a guest pays by bank transfer or in cash, use Mark as paid on the row. That stamps the settlement date and confirms the reservation, exactly as an online payment would. Open the record itself to note the method and a transaction reference. The Amount column totals at the bottom of the table, and the Settled payments only filter narrows it to real income.

8. Guests and reviews

Clients

People → Clients shows everyone who has booked: name, email, number of reservations, total paid, whether their email is verified, and the date they joined. It is your address book and your best-guest list in one table.

Reviews

Guests may review a stay after checking out. Reviews arrive pending and stay invisible to the public until you act on them, from People → Reviews.

  • Approve — publishes the review on the property page.
  • Hide from site — keeps it in the back office but off the public page.
  • Edit — fix an obvious typo, never the guest's opinion.
The dashboard card Reviews to moderate tells you when something is waiting. Approving quickly keeps the listing looking alive.

9. Everyday checklist

RhythmWhat to do
Every dayCheck the orange badge on Bookings; confirm or cancel anything pending. Approve new reviews.
Every weekWork through "Awaiting payment"; mark offline settlements as paid. Filter bookings by stay dates to prepare arrivals.
Every monthCompare revenue with the previous month. Refresh photos and prices; set properties inactive when they are not available.

PART 2For the guest

How to find a property, book it, pay for it, and manage your stay. You can browse freely; an account is only created when you make your first reservation.

10. Finding a place to stay

The home page features the newest properties. Select Properties to see them all and narrow the list with the search bar:

DestinationFree text matched against the title, city and address.
Check-in / Check-outYour dates. Check-out must be at least one night after check-in.
GuestsThe size of your party. Properties that sleep fewer are filtered out.
Max priceYour ceiling per night.

Select Search to apply, or Clear to start over. Your criteria travel with you to the property page, so the dates you searched for are already selected when you open a listing.

11. Reading a property page

A listing gathers everything you need before deciding:

  • Photos room by room — bedrooms, kitchens and bathrooms are shown separately, so the layout is clear before you arrive.
  • The nightly price and capacity — the rate, and the maximum number of guests.
  • Amenities and features — WiFi, workspace, washer, views, and the rest.
  • Location — the address and a map when the owner has provided coordinates.
  • Arrival times — check-in and check-out times when they are published.
  • Guest reviews — real reviews from people who stayed, published after the owner has reviewed them.

The availability calendar

The calendar colours every night so you can see what is possible at a glance: Open night can be booked, Booked night is already taken, Past night has gone by. Pick your arrival and departure, set the number of guests, and Kozy shows the number of nights and an estimated total before you commit to anything.

12. Reserving and creating your account

Select Reserve this stay. What happens next depends on you.

If you are already signed in

The reservation is created straight away and you land on your bookings page.

If this is your first booking

  1. Enter your name and email address.
  2. Kozy emails you a six-digit code. Type it into the site. The code is valid for 10 minutes — request a new one if it lapses.
  3. Choose a password for your new account.
  4. Your account is created and your reservation is placed at the same time. Nothing is lost along the way.

If you already have an account

Kozy recognises your email address and simply asks for your password. The dates you chose are kept aside and the reservation is created as soon as you sign in.

Your new reservation starts as Pending: the dates are held for you, and payment is the next step.
A reservation cannot be placed if the dates overlap an existing booking, if check-in is in the past, if the stay is shorter than one night, or if your party is larger than the property allows. Kozy explains which rule applies and lets you adjust.

13. Paying for your stay

  1. Open My bookings and find the reservation.
  2. Select Pay now. A secure card payment opens for the exact total, in the property's currency.
  3. Complete the card payment.
  4. Your reservation flips to Confirmed and the payment is stamped with its date.

Card details are handled by the payment provider — Kozy never sees or stores your card number. If the owner has agreed to a bank transfer or cash instead, they record the payment on their side and your reservation is confirmed the same way.

14. Managing your bookings

My bookings is your home base. A summary at the top counts your reservations, what is still to pay, and what you have already paid. Tabs sort everything for you:

AllEvery reservation you have ever made.
UpcomingStays that have not started yet.
Staying nowThe stay you are on today.
CompletedStays that are behind you.
CancelledReservations you or the owner dropped.

Each reservation card shows the property, the dates, the number of guests, the total and the payment state, and offers:

Pay nowSettle a reservation that is still unpaid.
Download receiptA PDF receipt for the booking — useful for expenses or a visa file.
View propertyReturn to the listing for the address, the map and the arrival times.
Cancel bookingDrop the reservation (see below).

15. Cancelling a reservation

Select Cancel booking and confirm in the dialog that appears. Cancellation is possible up until the day your stay begins, as long as the stay has not already started. Once the arrival date is behind you, the button disappears and you should contact the owner directly.

Cancelling releases the nights for other guests straight away. If the stay was already paid, any refund is arranged with the owner and appears as Refunded on the payment.

16. Leaving a review

Once your check-out date has passed, a review form appears on the reservation in My bookings. Give a rating out of five and write a few lines about the stay.

  • Only guests who actually stayed can review, and only once per stay.
  • Reviews are read by the owner before publication, so yours may take a little time to appear on the property page.

17. Your profile and security

ProfileUpdate your name and email address.
PasswordChange your password at any time. Kozy asks for the current one first.
Two-factor authenticationAdd a code from an authenticator app on top of your password, and keep the recovery codes somewhere safe.
PasskeysSign in with your fingerprint, face or device PIN instead of typing a password.
AppearanceChoose a light or dark interface, or follow your system setting.

Forgotten your password? Use Forgot password on the sign-in page and follow the link emailed to you. After several failed attempts, sign-in pauses for a short while — simply wait and try again.

18. Common questions

Do I need an account to browse?No. An account is created at your first reservation, in the same flow.
My verification code never arrived.Check the spam folder, confirm the address you typed, then request a new code. Codes expire after 10 minutes.
Why can I not pick those dates?Greyed-out nights are already booked or in the past. Pick another window, or another property.
How is the total worked out?Nightly rate × number of nights, in the property's currency. The estimate on the listing is what you will be charged.
When is my stay guaranteed?When the reservation shows Confirmed, which happens as soon as the payment is settled.
Where is my receipt?Download receipt on the reservation in My bookings, at any time.
A property vanished from the site.The owner has set it inactive. Reservations already made are unaffected.
